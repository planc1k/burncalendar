# Talking to one real user

Goal: 15 minutes with one person in Bangladesh who deals with fire or burning, one quote for the project
page, and one thing to change in the tool. Do this Sunday morning. One conversation is enough.

## Who to try (any one)
- Bangladesh Forest Department: a divisional forest officer, ideally Chittagong Hill Tracts or Sundarbans.
- Department of Environment: air-quality or brick-kiln monitoring staff.
- Fire Service and Civil Defence: a station or control-room officer.
- A university researcher in forestry, environmental science or remote sensing (BUET, DU, CU, SUST, IUB).
- An NGO working on air quality or land use (for example staff at an environment-focused organisation you can reach).

A teacher or relative who can introduce you beats a cold message.

## Opening message (edit to sound like you)
> Hello, I'm a student in Dhaka taking part in the NASA Space Apps Challenge this weekend. My team built a
> map of 26 years of satellite fire detections for Bangladesh and its neighbours. Could I ask you five short
> questions about how you track burning in your work? It takes about 15 minutes, by phone or message.

## Questions
1. When burning or fire becomes a problem in your area, how do you find out today?
2. Which months do you expect it, and has that changed over the years you have worked there?
3. If you could see whether this month is unusual compared with past years, what would you do differently?
4. (Show the page for their division.) Does this calendar match what you know? What looks wrong?
5. What would make you open this again next month?

## What to write down
- Name, role and organisation, and whether they agree to be quoted by name. If not, quote by role only.
- One sentence in their words about the problem or the tool. Do not polish it.
- Anything they say is wrong on the map. That is as useful as praise.

## Where it goes
- Project page, Detailed Description: one paragraph, "What a user told us", with the quote.
- If they flag an error, add it to the limits honestly. Judges read that as rigour.

Do not write a quote for them or imply an endorsement they did not give.
